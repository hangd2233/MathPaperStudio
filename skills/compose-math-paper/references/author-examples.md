# Synthetic writing examples

All examples on this page are invented for this project. No author's papers,
reading history, private annotations, or personal style profile are included.

## Result and bound

State the domain and bound, derive the nonnegative deficit, and identify equality.
Example: for real x and y with x+y=1, the identity (x-y)^2=1-4xy gives xy<=1/4.

## Framework and technical input

State the main result, isolate the exact technical lemma, prove the lemma under
its hypotheses, and then explain how it yields the main result. Do not invent
the missing lemma to make the outline appear complete.

## Open question and method

Separate a proved special case from a general question. State the obstacle and
the extra hypothesis that would be needed. A research plan is not a theorem.
