# General mathematical writing defaults

These are public project defaults, not a profile of any individual author.

- State objects, assumptions, quantifiers, ranges and conclusions precisely.
- Organize definitions, results and proofs by mathematical dependency.
- Explain the actual argument; do not hide missing steps behind "obvious".
- Mark unresolved gaps at the affected result and in the project issue notes.
- Preserve mathematical meaning during language-only revision.
- Keep imported research materials read-only unless their author requests changes.
- Do not invent proofs, citations, novelty claims or claims of complete classification.
- A compiled PDF is not certification of mathematical correctness.
- Authors may override stylistic defaults explicitly for their own project.
