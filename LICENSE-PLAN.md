# Source-release license scope

The owner approved source-only publication under the policy in LICENSING.md.
The combined desktop application and new MPS application contributions use
AGPL-3.0-or-later. Original upstream MIT grants and vendor MIT/Apache-2.0
notices are retained; they are not replaced or revoked.

The locked MuPDF.js 1.27.0 renderer permits AGPL version 3 or later. The full
AGPL text, dependency lockfiles and build scripts are included. The synthetic
demo and original geometric artwork use their included MIT notices.

This initial release contains source only. It does not distribute application
bundles, dependency WASM/font binaries, private manuscripts, reports or account
configuration. Binary distribution needs a separate corresponding-source and
dependency-notice review. Editing a document does not license the document
under the application's license.
